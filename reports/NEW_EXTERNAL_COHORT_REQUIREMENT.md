# Requirement for a new confirmatory external cohort

The existing 50-person, 100-ear manual-reference set has already been evaluated and
audited. It remains a locked redevelopment benchmark and cannot regain untouched
confirmatory status.

A future one-shot confirmatory cohort should:

- contain at least 30 new patients (60 ears / 180 SSC-HSC-PSC masks);
- preferably come from a later time period or a different institution;
- be proven unused for model, augmentation, loss, threshold, morphology, or checkpoint selection;
- keep reference labels inaccessible until M0-M3 selection and the acceptance rule are frozen;
- use macro Dice point estimate >=0.78 as the primary acceptance endpoint;
- report a patient-clustered 95% confidence interval and all structure/surface/topology metrics;
- prohibit parameter changes after the test, regardless of success or failure.
