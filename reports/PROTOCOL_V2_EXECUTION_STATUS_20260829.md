# Protocol V2 execution status

Date: 2026-08-29

## Completed

- Segmentation M1-M3 equal-budget 5-epoch pilots completed on the locked
  patient-level LS_SEG_200 split. M2 was selected using internal validation only.
- Frozen M2 evaluation on the already exposed same-institution Center 2 and
  Center 3 strata completed. Their pooled macro Dice was 0.6696 (95% CI
  0.6527-0.6867), below the prespecified 0.78 point-estimate target. These strata
  are not two independent hospitals and are not a new confirmatory external test.
- M0 external geometry reliability completed on 50 people, 100 ears and 300
  AI-manual pairs with 5,000 patient-clustered bootstrap repetitions. Continuous
  ICC(A,1), Bland-Altman bias/limits of agreement, MAE and relative error are
  reported; pass/fail remains blocked because no acceptance threshold was signed.
- Schema-only clinical/P-EBM eligibility audit completed for 73 variable rows.
